The Method

The OPT model, explored.

NASM's Optimum Performance Training model builds capacity in deliberate phases. Tap through the levels — the pyramid follows.

PHASE 01 · LEVEL — STABILIZATION

Earn control before load

Movement quality, joint stability, and muscular endurance first. Higher reps, controlled tempo — the base every later phase stands on.

12–20 repsSlow tempoBalance & core
PHASE 02 · LEVEL — STRENGTH

Strength endurance

Pair stable and heavier movements in supersets so newly-built strength keeps its control. The bridge from base to real load.

8–12 repsSupersetsModerate load
PHASE 03 · LEVEL — STRENGTH

Muscular development

Hypertrophy focus — accumulate volume to build the muscle that maximal strength and power will later express.

6–12 repsHigher volumeShort rest
PHASE 04 · LEVEL — STRENGTH

Maximal strength

Heavy load, low reps, full recovery. Trains the nervous system to recruit force — the ceiling that power sits on top of.

1–5 repsHeavy loadLong rest
PHASE 05 · LEVEL — POWER

Power

Move well-owned patterns fast. Rate of force development — strength made explosive, the phase closest to sport.

ExplosiveLow repsFull recovery

The Inputs

Recovery is programmed, not left over.

☾

Sleep

7+ hours as the target. Short nights lower the next day's training load — no negotiation.

◈

Fuel

Regular meals on lab days. I don't run experiments — or heavy sessions — hungry.

↻

Reset

Walks, stretching, and one lighter day before heavy weeks keep energy steady.
