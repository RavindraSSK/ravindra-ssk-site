## Final LinkedIn Post

This week’s biggest AI shift wasn’t a smarter model — it was everything models can now connect to.

**SSK AI Hub — AI Tech Briefing, Week 4** covers August 22–28 through one theme: AI is breaking out of the model. The important work happened at the interfaces — hardware, speech, Earth-scale data, GPUs, production runtimes, and trustworthy evaluation.

**1. Anthropic’s Model Hardware Standard**
A research-preview, model-agnostic interface for operating physical devices — with safety limits declared in the interface itself — aiming to do for hardware what MCP did for software tools.

**2. Qwen3.8-Flash-Next open weights**
Qwen released the weights and framed the model as a preview of its next architecture, giving the open community an early look at design decisions before they harden.

**3. DeepMind’s double-blind frontier evaluation**
A pilot where the developer cannot see the test content — an attack on benchmark contamination, and a step toward evaluation as infrastructure rather than vendor-reported scores.

**4. Gemini 3.5 Transcribe**
Speech treated as an interface, not just a transcript: formatted, multilingual, latency-focused transcription in the Gemini API.

**5. CUDA Python 1.0 and TensorRT Model Connect**
Stable, semver’d GPU APIs for the language AI already runs on — plus a two-command path from open checkpoints to native, Python-free TensorRT inference.

Taken together, the model is becoming the constant. What keeps changing is what it can touch — and how reliably we can measure it.
