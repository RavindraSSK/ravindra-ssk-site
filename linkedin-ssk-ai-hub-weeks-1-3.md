## Recommended LinkedIn Post

Three weeks of AI developments, brought together in one place.

I’ve been building **SSK AI Hub — AI Tech Briefing**, a weekly desk that follows the engineering side of the AI ecosystem: frontier models, LLMs, agents, coding agents, developer tools, infrastructure, multimodal systems, open-source releases, research, and applied systems.

The goal is not to chase every announcement. It is to track the releases and papers that change how systems are designed, shipped, or evaluated — then make those shifts easier to read for people who build with AI.

Each edition goes past the headline. For every story, I look at what actually changed, why it matters technically, where the practical impact shows up, what becomes possible in real systems, and what AI engineers, developers, and researchers should watch next.

SSK AI Hub is not just collecting AI headlines. It is an attempt to understand what a development changes — and what becomes possible because of it.

The first three editions of the SSK AI Hub AI Tech Briefing are now live — Weeks 1, 2, and 3.
